# Top Builder OS

**What**: The one system a 1–10 person trades shop runs its whole business on —
replaces ServiceTitan (front office) AND QuickBooks (books) with one owned
double-entry ledger. Intake → booking → dispatch → time/materials at true
cost → invoicing → bank rec → statements → payroll/tax → CPA hand-off, with
eight ALWAYS_HITL "helper" agents doing the paperwork.

**Who**: "Mike," 41, owner-operator roofer — estimates at the kitchen table at
11pm; his wife does the books. Zero training tolerance, no accounting jargon
on operator surfaces. Also his CPA (bookkeeper role, Advisor Console) and his
crew (field pages, phones, gloves, sunlight).

**Register**: product — the tool disappears into the task. Plain 4th-grade
language on every operator surface; figures verbatim from the ledger, always.

**Non-negotiables**: every existing route/behavior keeps working; WCAG 2.2 AA;
mobile-first (crew uses phones in the field); no dark patterns.
